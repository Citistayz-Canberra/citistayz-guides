Your Keys

What you'll receive
You'll receive a blue-tipped key for the apartment front door and a black remote fob. The remote acts as your swipe card at every card reader in the building, and the buttons on the remote control the car parking garage door.

Step 1
Enter the car park on Binara Street
Drive to 1 Binara Street, City (across from the Crown Plaza Hotel). You'll see a white garage door. Press the buttons on the black remote to open it, then drive down the ramp.
Step 2
At the bottom — turn right
At the bottom of the first ramp, turn right.
Step 3
Immediately turn left — down the next ramp
After the first right, take the immediate first left and go down the next ramp.
Step 4
Past the elevators — turn right, then right again
You'll pass the elevators. Turn right when you see the red car ahead, then immediately turn right again.
Step 5
Head straight — down the next ramp
Head straight and go down the next ramp. Turn right in front of the elevators, then immediately right again.
Step 6
Again — right past elevators, then right again
Turn right in front of the elevators, then immediately right again.
Step 7
Continue straight — space 408 on the left
Continue straight. Space 408 is on the left-hand side. You are now on Level B2.
Step 8
Park in space 408
Park in the space marked 408 on your left. The garage door will close automatically behind you.
Step 9
Take the lift to Level 4
Walk back to the last set of elevators you drove past on B2. Swipe the black remote fob on the reader and press Level 4. Apartment is 408.

Returning to your car: Use the fob in the elevator and select B2.
