About the property

A stylish, black and white themed 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment, nestled among the trees in leafy Braddon, giving you all the benefits of the location without the CBD noise. This ground floor apartment comes with 1 secure car space and a private front patio. Both bedrooms feature a queen bed, plus a comfortable sofa bed for extra guests. A 55 inch smart TV and a dedicated study desk make this a genuine option for work or travel. Guests consistently describe the location as calm, quiet, and convenient.

Property FAQs

Is the apartment cleaned during my stay?
Daily housekeeping isn't included. You can book a mid-stay clean right here in your portal.

Is linen included?
Yes. Linen is supplied for the number of guests on your booking.

What supplies are included?
A starter pack of kitchen, bathroom and cleaning supplies. Top-ups come with a mid-stay clean, or you're welcome to buy your own.

Can I get a tax invoice?
Yes. Email bookings@citistayz.com and we'll send one.

Can I smoke?
No. The apartment and patio are strictly non-smoking. A cleaning fee applies.

Quiet hours?
10:00pm to 8am. No parties or events.

Safety Protocols

🚨 In Case of an Emergency

If there is an emergency, please dial 000 immediately. Once it is safe to do so, notify our team so we can assist if needed.

🔔 Smoke Alarm

For your safety, the apartment is fitted with smoke alarms. Please do not cover or tamper with the smoke alarm under any circumstances.

When cooking, if you need extra air, please open the back sliding door.

🏃 Evacuation Procedure

In the event of an evacuation, leave through the back sliding door and move well away from the building.
