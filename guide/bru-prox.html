About the property

A ground floor 1 bedroom apartment in Proximity, a mixed-use complex in Bruce with its own cafe, restaurant and bar downstairs. The open living area opens onto a private, north-facing patio. There's a study nook, a large TV, and a sofa bed as well as the queen bed. Free secure parking is included. You're steps from the AIS and close to GIO Stadium, the University of Canberra and North Canberra Hospital.

Safety Protocols

🚨 In Case of an Emergency

If there is an emergency, please dial 000 immediately. Once it is safe to do so, notify our team so we can assist if needed.

Please follow all evacuation instructions provided via the building's speaker system.

🔔 Smoke Alarm

For your safety, the apartment is fitted with smoke alarms. Please do not cover or tamper with the smoke alarm under any circumstances.

If you need more air while cooking, please open the patio door.

🏃 Evacuation Procedure

In the event of an evacuation, follow the instructions from building staff or emergency services. Refer to the emergency evacuation map in the building, which shows all exits and the assembly area.
