About the property

A large, modern 3 bedroom home in the heart of Lyons. It's a 2 minute drive to Westfield Woden and about 10 minutes to almost anywhere in Canberra. There are 3 queen bedrooms plus a sofa bed in the living room. The kitchen has a butler's pantry, and there's a free-standing bath, central heating and cooling, a large Smart TV and fast free Wi-Fi. A portacot and highchair are available. Free driveway and street parking.

Safety Protocols

🚨 In Case of an Emergency

If there is an emergency, please dial 000 immediately. Once it is safe to do so, notify our team so we can assist if needed.

For your safety, the home is fitted with smoke alarms. Please do not cover or tamper with the smoke alarm under any circumstances.
