About the property

Just a short walk from Canberra Hospital, this 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom apartment is a calm place to rest. Warm red decor runs throughout. Relax in the spa bath, spread out in the open-plan living area, and unwind on the private balcony. There's a queen bed, a sofa bed, a large Smart TV and a fully equipped kitchen. It's on the first floor, close to CIT and Woden Town Centre, with free parking on site.

Property FAQs

Is the apartment cleaned during my stay?
Daily housekeeping isn't included. You can book a mid-stay clean right here in your portal.

Is linen included?
Yes. Linen is supplied for the number of guests on your booking.

What supplies are included?
A starter pack of kitchen, bathroom and cleaning supplies. Top-ups come with a mid-stay clean, or you're welcome to buy your own.

Can I get a tax invoice?
Yes. Email bookings@citistayz.com and we'll send one.

Can I smoke?
No. The apartment and balcony are strictly non-smoking. A cleaning fee applies.

Quiet hours?
10:00pm to 8:00am. No parties or events.

Safety Protocols

🚨 In Case of an Emergency

If there is an emergency, please dial 000 immediately. Once it is safe to do so, notify our team so we can assist if needed.

🔔 Smoke Alarm

For your safety, the apartment is fitted with smoke alarms. Please do not cover or tamper with the smoke alarm under any circumstances. If you need more air while cooking, open the balcony door.

🏃 Evacuation

In an emergency, leave by the stairs and move well away from the building.
