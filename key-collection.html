📍 How to Find Your Keys

Step 1
Find the Big Blue Canstay Sign
Head to Lonsdale Street, Braddon. Look for Greasy's Pizza on the corner, with Messina and Celebrations just to the right. We're right next door to On Lonsdale café. Look for the large blue Canstay sign on the corner of the gold building.
Step 2
Find the Lockboxes
Go to the large blue Canstay sign. The lockboxes are on the wall to the left of the sign.

🚗 Driving? You can pull up right next to the lockboxes to grab your keys, if traffic allows.
Step 3
Collect Your Keys
Locate your lockbox number and enter the access code — both are visible in your reservation guide from 3:00 PM on check-in day. Take your keys, then close the lockbox and scramble the code.
