Check-In Instructions

Select your route below

Step 1
Collect Your Keys
Keys are collected from 23 Lonsdale Street, Braddon. Visit citistayz.com/keycollection for full details.
Step 2
Enter Through the Foyer
Head to 19 Marcus Clarke Street, New Acton. Proceed straight through the foyer entry doors.
Step 3
Past the Peacock — Follow Signs to West Tower
Once inside, you'll pass the iconic peacock sculpture. Follow the signs to the West Tower Apartments.
Step 4
Proceed Down the Hallway — Turn Right
Proceed down the hallway and turn right, continuing to follow signs to the West Tower Apartments.
Step 5
Take the Lift to Level 11
Head to the Tower West elevators. Use your remote to activate the lift and select Level 11.
Step 6
Find Apartment 1106
Exit the lift on Level 11 and look for apartment 1106. Use your key to enter — enjoy your stay!
🎬  Parking Walkthrough Video
Step 1
Collect Your Keys
Keys are collected from 23 Lonsdale Street, Braddon. Visit citistayz.com/keycollection for full details.
Step 2
Drive to the Carpark Entry
Drive to the end of Marcus Clarke Street and follow the road around to the carpark entry.
Step 3
Open the Garage Door
Use the buttons on the remote to activate and open the garage door.
Step 4
Head Down to the Bottom Level
Proceed through the door and take the first left down the ramp. Continue left at every ramp — there are 4 ramps in total. Follow all the way down to the bottom level (B2). If unsure, refer to the video at the top of this page.
Step 5
Proceed to the End and Turn Right
Once at the bottom, proceed all the way to the end of the road and hook around to the right.
Step 6
Find Your Car Spaces — U1106
Your 2 allocated car spaces (U1106) are towards the back left corner — one on each side of the 2nd last pillar.
Step 7
Find the Elevators
The elevators are adjacent and to the right of your car space. You are on Level B2.
Step 8
Take the Lift to Level 11
Inside the lift, use your remote to activate the floors and select Level 11.
Step 9
Find Apartment 1106
Exit the lift on Level 11 and look for apartment 1106. Use your key to enter — enjoy your stay!
