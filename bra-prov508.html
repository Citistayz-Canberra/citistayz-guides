Check-In Instructions

Choose your check-in route below

Step 1
Make your way to Provenance
Head to 47 Currong Street North, Braddon. Use the buttoned remote as a swipe key to enter the building.
Step 2
Take the lift to Level 5
Head to the lifts and press Level 5. Swipe the blue fob on the panel inside the lift to access your floor.
Step 3
Turn right out of the lift
As you exit the lifts on Level 5, the apartment is to the right. Head down the hallway to apartment 508.
Watch First
Parking Walkthrough Video
Watch this short video for a full visual walkthrough of the car park route before following the steps below.
Step 1
Head to 2 Batman Street, Braddon
Go to the back of the building via Batman Street. You'll see the car park entry — use your remote to open the left-hand door.
Step 2
Turn left onto Batman Street
Turn left onto Batman Street as shown. The car park entry will be ahead of you.
Step 3
Go down the ramp — take the first right
Drive down the ramp and take the first right-hand turn.
Step 4
Take the second right
Continue and take the second right-hand turn (not the first).
Step 5
Drive to the end — turn left at B2 sign
Drive all the way to the end and turn left where the sign reads "Ramp Access to B2".
Step 6
Immediate right after the B2 sign
After turning left at the B2 sign, take an immediate right.
Step 7
Follow the arrow — turn left
Follow the direction shown and turn left inside the car park.
Step 8
Your car space — 508/47
Your car space 508/47 will be on your left-hand side. Park here.
Step 9
Head through the door to the lifts
Once parked, head through this door to access the lifts. You are on Level -1 — swipe your blue fob and press Level 5 to reach your apartment.
