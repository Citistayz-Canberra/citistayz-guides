Check-In Instructions

Choose your check-in route below

Step 1
Make your way to Valonia
Head to 17 Dooring Street, Braddon. You will see the Valonia building from the street.
Step 2
Your keys
Your keys look like this. Once you have retrieved them, the remote acts as a swipe card to open all doors and operate the lift throughout the building.
Step 3
Swipe the remote to enter the building
At the building entry, swipe the remote on the reader to open the automatic doors and gain access to the building.
Step 4
Find apartment 108
Go through the automatic doors and turn right. Walk past the lifts — apartment 108 is located on the ground floor in the right-hand corner of the building. This is your apartment door.
Step 1
Turn right onto Dooring Street
Make your way to Dooring Street, Braddon and turn right as shown.
Step 2
Turn right onto Lowanna Street
Continue and turn right onto Lowanna Street as shown.
Step 3
Open the gate with the remote
Head toward the car park gate. Use the buttons on the remote to open the gate. Note the low clearance of 2.15m.
Step 4
Turn right — drive to the end — ramp to B2
Once through the gate, turn right and drive straight all the way to the end. You will see a ramp down to Level B2 — turn left down the ramp.
Step 5
Find your car spaces — marked 108
Once down the ramp, drive straight to the end and turn left. You will soon see 2 tandem car spaces marked 108 on your right-hand side. Park here.
Step 6
Take the lift to Ground Floor
You are on Level B2. Swipe the remote on the reader in the lift and go up to Level G (Ground). Come out of the lift and turn left — apartment 108 is the first door on your right.
